📞 Phone Bill Calculator Spring Boot + REST API

Calculate the total cost of a phone bill based on call logs.

📖 How it works
• Each call line format: phoneNumber,startDateTime,endDateTime
• Date format: dd-MM-yyyy HH:mm:ss (example: 01-01-2025 08:00:00)
• Peak hours: 08:00 – 16:00 → rate 1.0 €/min
• Off‑peak hours → rate 0.5 €/min
• First 5 minutes of each call are charged at the corresponding rate.
• After 5 minutes, each additional minute costs 0.2 €/min (flat discount rate).
• The phone number with the highest call frequency gets all its calls for free (tie‑breaker: higher numeric value).
• The total bill excludes all charges from the promo number.
